🧼 Care Instructions

  • Wash before first use.
  • Wash immediately after use with mild soap and water.
  • Wipe dry immediately after washing.
  • Do not place in direct contact with fire or on a hot flame.
  • Avoid using sharp objects or knives on the mat.
  • Store in a clean and dry place.
